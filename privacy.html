Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

Dude Where Is My Car ("the App") is a free parking location tool. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server. All data stays on your device.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data in your browser's local storage (on your device only):

  • Parking location — GPS coordinates of where you parked
  • Parking details — floor, zone, spot number, and notes you enter
  • Parking photo — photo of your parking spot (if you choose to take one)
  • Parking history — your past parking locations
  • Favorite locations — spots you save as favorites
  • App settings — your preferences (voice, sound, auto-center)

This data is never sent to any server. You can clear all data at any time from the App's settings.

Permissions

  • Location (GPS) — required to mark your parking spot and navigate back. Your location is never transmitted to any server.
  • Camera — optional, only used if you choose to take a photo of your parking spot. Photos are stored locally on your device only.
  • Device Orientation — used for the compass feature during navigation.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services for map display and route calculation:

  • Leaflet / CartoDB — for map tiles (dark and satellite views). Their privacy policy: carto.com/privacy
  • OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine) — for walking route calculation. This is an open-source project. Only the start and end coordinates are sent to calculate the route.
  • ArcGIS / Esri — for satellite map tiles. Their privacy policy: esri.com/privacy

No personal information is shared with these services — only map coordinates necessary for displaying maps and routes.

Analytics & Advertising

The App does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services. There are no ads.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties. When you use the "Share" feature, you choose to share your parking location yourself via your device's native sharing options.

Data Deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time by going to Settings → "Clear all data" within the App, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
